Five branches, one set of numbers

A London restaurant group was logging end-of-day figures on paper at every site and estimating delivery commission by hand. Working out what the business had actually earned took about three hours a day at the busiest branches. Here is what replaced it.

The problem

Revenue arrived from four directions at once: dine-in, Uber Eats, Deliveroo and Just Eat. Each was tracked separately, so a real picture of performance meant piecing records together after the fact.

Commission percentages, VAT and platform ad spend were estimated rather than known. Petty cash and small daily expenses were not tracked at all, so there were gaps in the numbers that nobody could see.

Across five branches this was over ten hours a week spent reconciling figures instead of running the business.

What I built

Reporting

Standardised digital end-of-day reports from every branch, replacing paper.

Revenue

Consolidated takings from all delivery platforms and in-store sales in one view.

Costs

Commission and VAT calculated automatically rather than estimated; petty cash and daily expenses tracked properly.

Staffing

Rota system that learns each branch's scheduling pattern, forecasts staffing and reallocates cover when someone takes last-minute leave.

Alerts

Reports and operational notifications sent to managers on WhatsApp, where they already work.

Compliance

Menu and prep builder calculating calories and allergens whenever a new dish is added.

The result

Before After
3 hours a day of reconciliation at the busiest branches 30 minutes a week across the whole group
Commission and VAT estimated Calculated exactly, in real time
Petty cash untracked Full visibility of every small cost
No view of which branch performed best Post-commission profitability by branch

The core financial dashboard and rota system went live for testing within three weeks.

The point was never the dashboard. It was that the owner could stop reconciling figures and start deciding which branches to invest in.
