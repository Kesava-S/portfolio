Analytics Projects

Where is the London short-let market heading?

R, multiple linear regression

I built a regression model on London Airbnb listing data from 2024 and 2025 to estimate 2027 prices by listing category. Cleaning and preparing the dataset took most of the work. Selecting predictors and reading the coefficients told me which listing characteristics were associated with a higher price.

The output was an average price increase percentage per category, not a single market-wide number, so it showed where the market was moving by segment.

Why do two airlines with similar numbers get different reactions?

R, SEMrush, sentiment and text analysis

I collected Instagram and Facebook comments for Ryanair and Wizz Air through SEMrush, around 30 comments per post over two months in 2025, then cleaned and tokenised them in R and ran sentiment and text analysis.

Engagement figures tell you that people reacted. The comments tell you what they reacted to. Comparing the themes showed where the two audiences diverged, which no engagement metric would have surfaced.
